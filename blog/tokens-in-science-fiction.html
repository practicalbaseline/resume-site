Where "Tokens" Came From: Asimov to Attention Mechanisms

2026

When engineers talk about "tokens" in large language models, they mean discrete subword units that models process sequentially. But the word carries centuries of meaning — as a physical object exchanged for value, as a symbol standing in for something else, as a unit of access or permission. Science fiction picked up this lineage long before neural networks existed.

The original meaning

A token is a tangible object that represents something intangible. Roman soldiers carried tesserae as proof of identity. Medieval merchants used trade tokens when coinage ran short. In NLP, "understanding" might become ["under", "stand", "ing"] — the model never sees the word; it sees the tokens.

Tokens in Asimov's universe

Asimov never used "token" in the modern ML sense, but his work is saturated with discrete symbolic units that carry meaning, grant access, or encode rules.

The Three Laws as tokens of ethics

Sixty words — a fixed set of rules hardwired into every positronic brain. They don't describe intelligence; they constrain it.

A robot may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm.

Modern system prompts work the same way: fixed rules prepended to every request. Asimov wrote the first system prompt in 1942.

Multivac and insufficient data

In The Last Question (1956), successive supercomputers answer humanity's query across eons:

INSUFFICIENT DATA FOR MEANINGFUL ANSWER.

A token budget problem avant la lettre. The question demands more than any finite context window can hold.

Foundation's symbolic access

The Encyclopedia Galactica, the Prime Radiant, Hari Seldon's messages — all compress complex reality into discrete units that can be stored, transmitted, and acted upon.

From fiction to formalism

  1. 1950s–60s: Shannon's information theory — messages as discrete symbol sequences
  2. 1990s: Statistical NLP tokenizes text for parsing and translation
  3. 2016: BPE and SentencePiece popularize subword tokenization
  4. 2017: Transformers process token sequences with self-attention
  5. 2020s: "Token" becomes the billing unit and currency of the LLM economy

Why the metaphor matters

  • Tokens are stand-ins, not the thing itself. The model processes tokens, not meaning.
  • Tokens grant access. Your context window is a finite resource.
  • Tokens encode rules. System prompts are Asimov's Three Laws.
  • Insufficient data is a real answer. Sometimes the right response is to say you don't have enough context.
