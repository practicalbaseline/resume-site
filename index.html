Philosophy

How I think, work, and believe

Three pillars that shape everything I build — from code to conversations with subscribers.

01

How I think

I reason from first principles and think in futures, not just present states. Science fiction trained me to ask "what will the world look like?" before I write a single line of code. I test ideas against reality, revise when evidence demands it, and treat every system as something that will outlive its first deployment.

02

How I work

I show up every day. I write things down. I ship incrementally, measure honestly, and never confuse motion with progress. Problems are inevitable; solutions are not — they require deliberate, persistent effort. I learn continuously because the only education that counts is the kind you give yourself.

03

What I believe

Technology should elevate human dignity, not replace human judgment. I do not fear computers — I fear the lack of them. Change is the dominant force of our era, and the people who build the future must learn to think in science-fictional terms: anticipating consequences, designing for ethics, and building tools that are wisely used.
